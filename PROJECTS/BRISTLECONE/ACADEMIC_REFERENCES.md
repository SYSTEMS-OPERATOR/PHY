## Current conclusion

Bristlecone is viable as an offline endurance-design reference. It is not yet viable as a full fabrication-material specification.

Current evidence supports:

- partial species-level density and specific-gravity anchoring
- wood anatomy and age-related growth inquiry
- ecological adversity and longevity hypothesis
- drought tolerance and long-duration physiological persistence
- dendrochronological value

Current evidence does not yet support:

- Janka hardness
- modulus of rupture
- modulus of elasticity
- crushing strength
- shrinkage split
- 12 percent moisture-content engineering table
- fabrication authorization for actual ancient material

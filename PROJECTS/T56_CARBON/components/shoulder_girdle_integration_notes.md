# Shoulder-Girdle Integration Notes

Status: `CONCEPT_SCAFFOLD_NOT_FABRICATION_READY`

The floating scapular girdle is subordinate to the T-5.6 nested thorax and does not redefine universal PHY anatomy.

Integration rules:

- inherit the thoracic coordinate frame
- terminate primary arm reactions in the inner thoracic housing and segmented spine
- keep the secondary carbon ribcage outside the primary shoulder load path
- preserve independent left and right service access
- keep carriage, linkage, cartridge, sensor, guard, and fascia interfaces project-local
- leave all dimensional, material, load, range, and safety values unresolved until validated

The first engineering article should be a single-side root-to-dummy-humerus bench assembly. Paired shoulder integration follows only after the single-side trajectory, stops, retention, routing, and service procedure pass.

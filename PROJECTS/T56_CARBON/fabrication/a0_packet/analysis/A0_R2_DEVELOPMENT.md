# A0-R2 development record

Directive PHY-DEV-A0-R2-001 was authorized on 2026-10-21. Baseline main:
`02d5758d476e9ff753c1dcbb9951c2bce542fe88`. No physical work is authorized by this packet.

Reproduced before editing: each R1 yoke intersects its carriage by
1463.152224 mm³; R1 bearing cap opening equals bearing OD, with zero retaining
overlap. Focused regressions were added before correcting these defects.

The bounded load path is: tube → offset hub → radial shaft/bearing reactions →
yoke pockets and feet → carriage → four-bar/root fixture. Pitch-stop torque
travels directly from an integral hub lug to pads and their bolted carrier;
it does not travel as a shaft-axis torque through two radial bearing reactions.
The shaft/key still transmit index/friction torque and directional handling
loads. Those are separate free bodies. Bearing-center spacing reacts moments
about X/Z, not a moment about Y.

R2 preserves the pitch center, P/A/B/C axes, 317 mm station, 327 mm tube, mission
loads, design factors, canon and all anatomical/reference decisions. Only the
carriage's plate layer and cartridge manufacturing geometry change. The
carriage moves above the rocker layer and gains a central sweep relief; yokes
have explicit outboard feet, threaded fasteners and bearing pockets. Part
counts are consequences, never acceptance criteria.

All dimensions are engineering candidates pending independent review. The
sliding shaft fit, delivered bearing clearance, friction coupling, stop
compliance, remaining scapular hardware, guarding and bench support remain
explicit release holds. Digital contact or arithmetic cannot approve them.

The final interval audit includes locating-ring pocket travel in both directions.
The floating yoke has a15 mm pocket with a1.25 mm nose cap; nominal inward1 /
outward0.75 mm freedom avoids forcing a short center stack against its ledge.
End capture uses two distinct selective shim types, with the actual selection
left null until the complete delivered stack and shaft are measured.

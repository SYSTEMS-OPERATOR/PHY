# A0-R2 stop evidence request — proposed for independent review

**NOT RUN.** Do not substitute this proposal for the guarded article test plan.
The existing 2 J service / 6 J design-energy basis and four-millimeter criterion
remain unchanged. No compression curve or force/travel measurement is present.

First measure the installed angular window from initial compliant contact to the
rigid backing limit. At R60, a two-degree command-to-limit window permits only
about 2.094 mm tangential motion. R2's nominal first contact is already at the
hard limit, so it grants zero credited compliant travel. Resolve this geometry
and the required four millimeters before selecting any absorber or machining
its final locating features. Do not silently widen the allowed range or reduce
energy, factors or measured acceptance criteria.

The smallest useful physical input is a force/displacement curve for the actual
candidate pad thickness, contact width, material grade, temperature and support,
plus installed first-contact/backed-stop measurements. A generic acetal material
label cannot supply it.

For a qualified laboratory/reviewer to adapt: compress a documented pad coupon
between the actual-radius steel lug and flat backed support in a guarded,
calibrated load frame. Record load and displacement synchronously, fixture
compliance separately, and integrate F dx after subtracting fixture compliance.
Record specimen dimensions, material/lot, temperature, preload, speed, three
loading/unloading cycles, residual set, dwell/creep and observations. Stop at
fixture force/travel limits established by the responsible test engineer; this
packet does not authorize a load-frame operation.

A quasistatic curve alone cannot qualify a shock absorber. Subsequent reviewed
rate/temperature tests must address dynamic stiffness, energy absorbed versus
returned, rebound, repeated impacts, aging, fracture and bottoming. Match these
data to a proposed contact setting and repeat all six article load cases. Retain
raw data and uncertainty; no scalar spring constant may conceal a hard bottom.

Pass only after an independent reviewer reconciles the available motion,
required energy and acceptance limits, approves peak loads and load paths, and
issues the corresponding drawing/procurement revision. Keep every measurement
null and every test not_run until an actual signed record exists.

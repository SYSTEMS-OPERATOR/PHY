## Source scope and limits

- **calcaneus_ct_2024:** [Koh et al., Morphometric Analysis of the Calcaneus in a Southeast Asian Population](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11116927/). Revision: PMC11116927; Cureus (2024), published article at cited DOI. 34 female patients of 100 at Changi General Hospital, Singapore; skeletally immature patients and measured calcaneus fracture/surgery excluded.
  Table 2 female means/SD; Table 1 measurement sites. Side not separated; female marginal means used only as explicitly pooled engineering references. Body width and lateral-wall height are localized spans, not full-bone width/thickness.
- **cervical_ct_2021:** [Ekizoglu et al., Virtual morphometric method using seven cervical vertebrae for sex estimation on the Turkish population](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8354927/). Revision: PMC8354927.1; Int J Legal Med (2021), published article at cited DOI. Female training subset of 210 training subjects from 294 total (148 female); female mean age 44.76 +/- 12.94 for total cohort; Turkish trauma CT, cervical pathology excluded. Female training n not separately confirmed.
  Table 2 female training means/SD; validation columns not mixed; canal AP/TR kept distinct from bone widths. C1 has no centrum; study supplies no C1 HT. C2 HT is retained but not adopted pending confirmation of odontoid/height semantics. Canal void dimensions are not solid bone dimensions; no full envelope or joint center provided.
- **lumbar_ct_2022:** [Kot et al., Morphometric analysis of the lumbar vertebrae and intervertebral discs in relation to abdominal aorta: CT-based study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8917002/). Revision: PMC8917002; Surg Radiol Anat (2022), published article at cited DOI. 253 female patients of 557 abdominal CT examinations; overall cohort mean age 55.81 +/- 17.77; Krakow, Poland; 2016 acquisition.
  Table 1 Female group, L1-L5 upper/lower margins; disc and vascular rows excluded. Body and margin spans only; posterior elements and complete vertebral extents remain unknown. Do not combine marginal means into a measured donor or SOPHY registration.
- **patella_caliper_2013:** [Olateju, Philander and Bidmos, Morphometric analysis of the patella and patellar ligament of South Africans of European ancestry](https://sajs.co.za/article/download/4002/5775). Revision: S Afr J Sci 109(9/10), article 2013-0069 (2013). 25 female teaching cadavers; mean age 77.3 years (47-98); South African, European ancestry; bilateral dissection.
  Table 4 female means/SD; measurement definitions in Table 1; pooled height/width and separate thickness by side. Older preserved-cadaver cohort; not evidence of a 28-year-old target. Height/width are pooled over sides; only thickness is side-specific. Table 4 prints 'Patella weight' in a millimeter row. Interpreted as PW (width) using Table 1, methods and Table 6 width cross-check; correction is explicit.
- **rib1_caliper_2022:** [Arlegi et al., Sexual dimorphism in the first rib of Homo sapiens](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9005670/). Revision: PMC9005670; J Anat (2022), published article at cited DOI. Adult Euro-American skeletal sample; per-measure female side n in Table 3 (31-33 for selected rows); geometric-morphometry subset is distinct.
  Table 3 female R/L columns; Table 1 measurement definitions; chord/arc and shaft sites kept explicit. TVC/TVA begin at tubercle and exclude neck/head; not total rib length. Midshaft diameters at subclavian groove are not whole-rib extents; cartilage excluded.
- **talus_ct_2016:** [He et al., Three-dimensional Computer-assisted Modeling of Talus Morphology in Chinese Patients](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6584390/). Revision: PMC6584390.1; Orthop Surg 8(3), 383-392 (2016). 32 female volunteers; mean age 41.2 years (21-58); mean height 163.4 cm (156-173); bilateral CT, Tianjin, China.
  Female Left/Right columns of Table 1; methods section on sagittal/coronal apex spans inspected separately. Single-center cohort; means are not one donor, F28 or female_21_baseline. Original images/meshes unavailable for independent landmark or surface review. Do not adopt the dimensionally suspicious published volume entries.
- **thoracic_caliper_2011:** [Singh et al., Morphometric Measurements of Cadaveric Thoracic Spine in Indian Population and Its Clinical Applications](https://www.asianspinejournal.org/upload/pdf/asj-5-20.pdf). Revision: PMC3047895.1; Asian Spine J 5(1), 20-34 (2011). 19 female cadaveric vertebral columns of 100; overall mean age 52.8 (22-70); Indian institution; preserved bone sets.
  PDF page 7 / printed page 26, Table 3, female anterior/posterior height columns; T11 posterior height quarantined. Small female cohort, preserved specimens; no full vertebral shape. T11 posterior height conflicts with narrative and resembles Table 2 chord length. Retained as rejected native evidence; no corrected measurement inferred.

## Unresolved evidence

The 24 spine identities retain their original whole-bone length/width/thickness unknowns. Body/endplate heights and canal void spans are independently named. C1 has no body height, C2 HT has unresolved odontoid semantics, and the inconsistent T11 posterior-height entry is quarantined.

Calcaneal body/wall spans do not resolve whole-bone width/thickness. First-rib tubercle spans exclude neck/head, so they do not become total rib length. Ribs 2 and the naviculars have no admitted three-span defaults. Existing sacrum, sternum, hips and long-bone maps remain legacy declarations; they are not promoted by this work.

The source review, NASA crosswalk and NIH candidate disposition are in docs/PHY-DIM-072.md. No unqualified mesh measurements are used.

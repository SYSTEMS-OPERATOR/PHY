## Qualitative feature declarations

- **BONE_C1**: Ring formed by anterior and posterior arches; no vertebral body or spinous process. Superior articular facets for the occipital condyles; inferior facets for the axis. Transverse foramina in the transverse processes.
- **BONE_C2**: Dens (odontoid process) projects superiorly from the body. Atlas-dens articulation is supported by the transverse ligament. Transverse foramina; neural arch and articular processes.
- **BONE_C3**: Vertebral body and neural arch enclose the vertebral foramen. Transverse processes contain transverse foramina. Short spinous process, commonly bifid; individual morphology varies.
- **BONE_C4**: Vertebral body and neural arch enclose the vertebral foramen. Transverse processes contain transverse foramina. Short spinous process, commonly bifid; individual morphology varies.
- **BONE_C5**: Vertebral body and neural arch enclose the vertebral foramen. Transverse processes contain transverse foramina. Short spinous process, commonly bifid; individual morphology varies.
- **BONE_C6**: Vertebral body and neural arch enclose the vertebral foramen. Transverse processes contain transverse foramina. Short spinous process, commonly bifid; individual morphology varies.
- **BONE_C7**: Prominent, elongated spinous process at the cervicothoracic transition. Transverse foramina; vertebral body, neural arch and articular processes.
- **BONE_T1**: Whole superior costal facets for rib 1 and inferior demifacets for rib 2. Transverse costal facets for the rib-1 tubercles.
- **BONE_T2**: Costal body demifacets and transverse costal facets for rib articulation. Pedicles and laminae form the neural arch around the vertebral foramen. Posterior spinous process; its size and inclination vary with level.
- **BONE_T3**: Costal body demifacets and transverse costal facets for rib articulation. Pedicles and laminae form the neural arch around the vertebral foramen. Posterior spinous process; its size and inclination vary with level.
- **BONE_T4**: Costal body demifacets and transverse costal facets for rib articulation. Pedicles and laminae form the neural arch around the vertebral foramen. Posterior spinous process; its size and inclination vary with level.
- **BONE_T5**: Costal body demifacets and transverse costal facets for rib articulation. Pedicles and laminae form the neural arch around the vertebral foramen. Posterior spinous process; its size and inclination vary with level.
- **BONE_T6**: Costal body demifacets and transverse costal facets for rib articulation. Pedicles and laminae form the neural arch around the vertebral foramen. Posterior spinous process; its size and inclination vary with level.
- **BONE_T7**: Costal body demifacets and transverse costal facets for rib articulation. Pedicles and laminae form the neural arch around the vertebral foramen. Posterior spinous process; its size and inclination vary with level.
- **BONE_T8**: Costal body demifacets and transverse costal facets for rib articulation. Pedicles and laminae form the neural arch around the vertebral foramen. Posterior spinous process; its size and inclination vary with level.
- **BONE_T9**: Costal-facet pattern is transitional; an inferior demifacet may be absent. Vertebral body, neural arch, transverse and spinous processes.
- **BONE_T10**: Costal body facets for rib 10; facet configuration varies. Transverse costal-facet pattern is not assumed from typical thoracic levels.
- **BONE_T11**: Single pair of complete costal facets for rib 11. No transverse costal facets.
- **BONE_T12**: Single pair of complete costal facets for rib 12; no transverse costal facets. Transitional thoracic superior and lumbar-type inferior articular morphology.
- **BONE_L1**: Large vertebral body with pedicles, laminae and vertebral foramen. Short, blunt spinous process; transverse and articular processes. Superior articular facets face medially; inferior facets face laterally.
- **BONE_L2**: Large vertebral body with pedicles, laminae and vertebral foramen. Short, blunt spinous process; transverse and articular processes. Superior articular facets face medially; inferior facets face laterally.
- **BONE_L3**: Large vertebral body with pedicles, laminae and vertebral foramen. Short, blunt spinous process; transverse and articular processes. Superior articular facets face medially; inferior facets face laterally.
- **BONE_L4**: Large vertebral body with pedicles, laminae and vertebral foramen. Short, blunt spinous process; transverse and articular processes. Superior articular facets face medially; inferior facets face laterally.
- **BONE_L5**: Large vertebral body with pedicles, laminae and vertebral foramen. Short, blunt spinous process; transverse and articular processes. Superior articular facets face medially; inferior facets face laterally.
- **BONE_SACRUM**: Typically five fused sacral segments; one adult inventory identity. Sacral promontory and auricular surfaces for articulation with the ilia. Sacral canal, sacral hiatus and paired anterior/posterior sacral foramina.
- **BONE_COCCYX**: Usually four coccygeal segments, with three to five observed; fusion is variable. Sacrococcygeal and intercoccygeal interfaces vary in fusion and alignment. Straight and curved lengths are different anatomical measurements.
